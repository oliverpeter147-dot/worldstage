How it works & limitations

WorldStage processes audio played inside the app, or an explicitly selected live input. It does not automatically change all Windows audio. A virtual audio cable is needed for other applications; route their output into the cable, then choose the cable as this app’s input. Keep WorldStage’s output on your speakers or headphones, never on the same cable.

Stadiums use estimated virtual geometry with delayed stand reflections, roof enclosure and audience absorption. Other venues use category-based synthetic reverb. No bundled preset is a measured acoustic replica. You can import your own measured impulse response. Festivals refer to event settings rather than fixed buildings. The library is curated and not exhaustive. Create custom presets for any missing location. Custom venues and settings are saved locally; no account or internet is needed. Audio files stay on your PC. Supported formats depend on the bundled Chromium codecs; WAV and MP3 are recommended.
