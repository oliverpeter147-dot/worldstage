How it works & limitations

WorldStage processes audio played inside the app, or an explicitly selected live input. It does not automatically change all Windows audio. A virtual audio cable is needed for other applications; route their output into the cable, then choose the cable as this app’s input. Keep WorldStage’s output on your speakers or headphones, never on the same cable.

All named venues use estimated, category-based synthetic reverb, not measured acoustic replicas. Festivals refer to event settings rather than fixed buildings. The library is curated and not exhaustive. Create custom presets for any missing location. Custom venues and settings are saved locally; no account or internet is needed. Audio files stay on your PC. Supported formats depend on the bundled Chromium codecs; WAV and MP3 are recommended.
